When an artist creates a piece, they view it through the lens of **intention, emotion, and process**. The audience, however, engages with the final result without access to that internal experience. This leads to a difference in interpretation and appreciation.

## Key Concepts

- **Intentional Fallacy**  
  The idea that a work should not be judged solely by the creator’s intention. The audience's interpretation stands independently.

- **Authorial Distance**  
  The emotional and cognitive gap between the creator's closeness to the work and the audience's detached perspective.

- **Reception Theory**  
  Focuses on how meaning is created by the audience, not just the artist. Different viewers may interpret the same piece in varied ways.

- **Empathy Gap in Art** *(informal)*  
  The audience may not fully empathize with the emotional context of creation, leading to different responses.

> 🎨 **Summary:**  
> The difference in how an artwork is perceived by its creator versus its audience stems from a complex interplay of intention, context, and individual interpretation.

One way is clearly see it is by writing something that you feel strongly emotional about, and reading it after sometime when you are not that emotionally synced to it. You can now see it in action(only partially as you are still aware of the context and emotions when you wrote it).
