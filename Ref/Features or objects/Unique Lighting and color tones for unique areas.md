Some areas have unique lighting and color tones
This is very important in places like certain temples
Eg:
1. some temples have more diyas leading to saffron tint + dark areas looking more dark
2. Some temples(like Tiruttani interior) have a lot of ash(vibudhi/ lime) leading to greyish white tint
3. Some have unique tiles leading to a saturated tinting
