
## State of play
In Hades there are play styles vary a lot depending upon the weapon you choose.
1. The Spear demands you to attack in a fast pace mode it demands more attacks in succession hence making your decision making spontaneous this fast paced state of play does put you in a **state of chaos** and very **impulsive**.
2. The bow on the other hand is pretty much the opposite. You physically can't button match to have successive attacks but if you time your attack well  the result is pretty good and comparable to the spear. This physically stop you to have a fast paced experience and and demands strategic gameplay and puts you in a **calming state of mind** and **decisive**.
How you experience other parts of story will be very highly dependent on this.
