Some ares can have very small floating islands. The Small islands need some kind of transport. It can be of either two types: 
1. Can support high traffic and easy access
2. Very difficult to travel through and only a few can access.

Just for artistic effects there can be waterfalls and rivers flowing through. These rivers can also be considered holy in certain scenarios and there might be some temples that are associated with it or could be the source or centre of the scene or culture around it.
