Any game made is always compared with other games it pieces of art. There is significance in this as this gives familiarity to the audience. 

this comparison can degrade any experience if not used well. But if it is used well, it can give sense of familiarity and upon that if the game gives good experience its just GOLD.

Example: Expedition 33. It has elements of many games like persona, final fantasy, assasins creed, last of us. That gives familiarity, but everything is implemented well(especially music, gameplay, combat, cutscenes, voice acting, writing, etc) and the game is good. So it's gold.
