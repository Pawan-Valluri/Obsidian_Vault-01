If something is to be prevented, if penalizing Measures are taken, there will be backlash. Rather if a encouragement is given, it's taken well.

### Example
in world of warcraft, the player were playing too long, even more than 24 hours at a strech. 

#### Penalty
To prevent this initially a penalizing measure was taken where the players were given a penalized Exp modifier. This was taken very badly by the players. Even resulting in many quitting.

#### Encouragement measure
The studio revised the measure to rather than penalty an encouraging boon was given. 
An exp boon was given to all players, which grew as players were logged out. This moved players to limit their session length, as staying logged out was a advantage.

### The darker take to this concept
