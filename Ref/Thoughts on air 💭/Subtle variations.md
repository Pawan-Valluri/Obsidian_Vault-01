#NaturalMotion


Every movement in a game is very deterministic. But that feels very unrealistic.
Adding subtle variations(should be context dependant in almost all cases) to various aspects of Characters like movement, speech, interactions with environment, player and other characters, etc could feel very natural.

RDR2 was doing a very good job for the player character and NPCs for this.

Can look into motion blending and dynamic motion synthesis. Well known tools are Euphoria and endorphin by natural motion. This is used in GTA 4 & 5 and RDR 1 & 2.
