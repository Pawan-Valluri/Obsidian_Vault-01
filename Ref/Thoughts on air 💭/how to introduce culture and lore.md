In games like Baldur's Gate 3 and Witcher 3, the NPCs do have culture and historical significance. The game developers introduced their culture in a paced manner through the means of lore, speech patterns, values, dress, jewelry, etc. More importantly any term that is mentioned, you can learn what it is, through dialogue choices, item descriptions, NPC reactions to it(like baahubali), digging through things yourself, or the story itself shows it. 

In games like Ghost of Tsushima, you are introduced to Japanese culture. You can understand their culture through the above things too but also as this culture actually exists in real life, actors acted it well(as it feels in-between acting and living). Actors can act more nuanced and subtle stuff that can go unnoticed by players. 


This has to be done well by the voice actors and depending on their own culture.
